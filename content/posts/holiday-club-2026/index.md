## 🪐 Join Mission Control!

This summer, we're blasting off on an unforgettable **Space Mission** as we follow the adventures of Paul and discover how Jesus built his church across the world.

But this isn't a mission to discover new planets. It's an even greater mission—a mission to take the good news about Jesus to people everywhere!

As members of **Mission Control**, we'll follow Paul's journey through the book of Acts and discover an amazing truth:

### ✨ Nothing can stop Jesus building his kingdom!

## 🎭 Follow Paul's Adventures!

Throughout the week, our drama team will help us follow Paul's exciting journey to Macedonia from Acts 16. Along the way we'll meet fascinating characters, face unexpected challenges, and see how the good news of Jesus spreads despite opposition.

We'll discover that:

* 🚀 Jesus sends his people on mission.
* 🌍 The gospel is for people from every background.
* ⚡ Opposition cannot stop God's plans.
* 👑 Jesus is building his kingdom.
* ⭐ And we can be part of his amazing mission!
