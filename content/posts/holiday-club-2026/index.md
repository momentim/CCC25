# 🚀 Ready for Launch?

Get ready for an exciting journey through the stars as we discover the greatest mission ever!

# 🌟 SPACE MISSION HOLIDAY CLUB 🌟

## 🪐 Join Mission Control!

This summer, we're blasting off on an unforgettable **Space Mission** as we follow the adventures of Paul and discover how Jesus built his church across the world.

But this isn't a mission to discover new planets. It's an even greater mission—a mission to take the good news about Jesus to people everywhere!

As members of **Mission Control**, we'll follow Paul's journey through the book of Acts and discover an amazing truth:

## ✨ Nothing can stop Jesus building his kingdom!

People opposed Paul. They threatened him, imprisoned him, and tried to silence the message of Jesus. But again and again, the good news spread, lives were transformed, and God's church grew.

And the most exciting part of all?

**Jesus is still building his kingdom today—and you can be part of his amazing mission!**

---

# 🚀 Our Mission This Week

## 🚀 Day 1 – Mission Launch *(Acts 9)*

Paul was once an enemy of Jesus, but everything changed when Jesus met him on the road to Damascus. Through Ananias, Paul discovered that Jesus had chosen him for a special mission—to take the good news to people everywhere.

**Mission Focus:** Jesus changes lives and sends his people on mission.

---

## 🌍 Day 2 – Mission Expands *(Acts 11)*

The good news about Jesus spreads beyond God's ancient people to people from many different nations and backgrounds. In Antioch, the first church made up mostly of Gentiles is born, showing that God's kingdom is open to all who trust in Jesus.

**Mission Focus:** The gospel is for everyone!

---

## ⚡ Day 3 – Mission Under Attack *(Acts 19)*

In Ephesus, the message of Jesus changes so many lives that it causes a huge uproar! Many oppose the gospel, but nothing can stop God's plans or prevent Jesus from building his church.

**Mission Focus:** Opposition cannot stop Jesus building his kingdom.

---

## 🎉 Day 4 – Mission Success *(Acts 16)*

We'll travel with Paul to Philippi and meet Lydia, a businesswoman; a slave girl trapped in darkness; and a jailer who thought all hope was lost. Three very different people, one amazing Saviour!

We'll discover that God's good news is for everyone and that Jesus is still growing his kingdom today.

**Mission Focus:** Jesus saves all kinds of people and invites us into his kingdom.

---

## 🎭 Follow Paul's Adventures!

Throughout the week, our drama team will help us follow Paul's exciting journey to Macedonia from Acts 16. Along the way we'll meet fascinating characters, face unexpected challenges, and see how the good news of Jesus spreads despite opposition.

We'll discover that:

* 🚀 Jesus sends his people on mission.
* 🌍 The gospel is for people from every background.
* ⚡ Opposition cannot stop God's plans.
* 👑 Jesus is building his kingdom.
* ⭐ And we can be part of his amazing mission!
