We’re starting a **new community music group** at Charleston Community Church, and everyone is invited.

The **Charleston Music Group** is a relaxed and friendly space for people who enjoy singing or playing instruments to come together and make music. There are no auditions and no pressure — just encouragement, community, and the joy of making music with others.
{{< figure src="cmg.png" >}}

Whether you’re experienced, a little rusty, or just starting out, you are very welcome.

### When & Where
**Fridays | 7–9pm Starts 30th January tbd**  
**Charleston Community Church**

### Cost
**Free**

If you’ve ever wanted to play music with others in a welcoming environment, this is a great place to start. Come along and join us.
